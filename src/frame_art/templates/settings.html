Filling the screen

A work that isn't 16:9 is either cropped to fill the screen or shown whole on a background. The crop level sets how much a crop may cut off; past that the work gets a background. It applies to everything on the TV: museum works, your photos, portraits and versions. Compare the levels.

The pool on the TV

Each night retires the works on the TV longest and adds new ones from the Library, as the Schedule mixes them. The picture interval takes effect the next time you press "Show frame-art's works" or "Back to Samsung art" on the overview.

Nightly run

Home Assistant uploads new works only while the TV is on or in Art Mode, so a run at night never wakes the screen.
